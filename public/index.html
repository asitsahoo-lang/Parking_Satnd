About ParkSmart Digital

ParkSmart Digital is a digital parking management solution that replaces paper slips with electronic tickets and allows parking stand operators to collect fees securely via UPI QR codes and UPI payment links. The platform is built for parking stand agents to issue, verify, and close tickets in real time, with automatic fee calculation and instant payment confirmation — removing the need for cash handling or manual receipt books.

ParkSmart Digital operates as a centralized management system for multiple parking locations and stands, operated by agents under a single business owner. Each location's tickets, collections, and settlements are tracked in one place.

How It Works

1. Vehicle Entry

An agent creates a digital ticket with a photo and entry time — no paper slip needed.

2. Auto-Calculated Fee

At exit, the parking duration and fee are calculated automatically — never entered manually.

3. Secure UPI Payment

Customers pay instantly via a live UPI QR code or a shareable payment link.

4. Instant Ticket Closure

The ticket closes automatically the moment payment is confirmed, with a full digital record.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Since the service (parking) is provided immediately upon payment at the exit, fees are generally non-refundable once the ticket is closed. However, in cases of technical double-deductions or payment failures where the amount is debited but the ticket remains open, refunds will be processed back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.

Customers who believe they have been incorrectly charged may reach out using the contact details below, and our team will investigate and resolve the issue promptly.

Privacy Policy

ParkSmart Digital collects only the information necessary to operate its parking ticket and payment system, including vehicle entry/exit details, ticket photos for verification purposes, and payment transaction records. Payment processing is handled securely through Razorpay, and ParkSmart Digital does not store any card, UPI, or bank account credentials on its own servers.

Data collected is used solely for issuing tickets, calculating fees, processing payments, and maintaining transaction records for accounting and support purposes. Information is not sold or shared with third parties beyond what is required to process payments through our payment gateway partner.

Terms of Service

By using ParkSmart Digital at any of our operated parking locations, customers agree to pay the applicable parking fee, calculated based on the duration their vehicle is parked. Fees are displayed prior to payment and are collected via UPI at the time of vehicle exit. Parking stand operators and agents using this platform agree to accurately verify vehicles before closing tickets and to use the payment collection features solely for legitimate parking fee transactions.
